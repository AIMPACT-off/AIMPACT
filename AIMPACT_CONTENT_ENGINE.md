# AIMPACT CONTENT ENGINE

## OBJECTIVE

Create a repeatable system that converts AIMPACT's actual intelligence and execution work into distribution and qualified business demand.

## MASTER PIPELINE

**QUESTION**
→ **RESEARCH**
→ **EVIDENCE**
→ **DECISION**
→ **WORKFLOW**
→ **TEST**
→ **RESULT**
→ **CONTENT**
→ **DISTRIBUTION**
→ **TRAFFIC**
→ **BUSINESS PROBLEM**
→ **LEAD**
→ **PAID PILOT**
→ **OUTCOME**
→ **CASE STUDY**
→ **NEW CONTENT**

---

## 1. CONTENT RECORD

Every meaningful content item should have:

- Content ID
- Source research/workflow ID
- Business problem
- Target industry
- Target persona
- Evidence status
- Primary claim
- Supporting evidence
- Format
- Channel
- CTA
- Published date
- UTM/campaign identifier
- Qualified traffic
- Lead count
- Pipeline value
- Revenue influence
- Outcome/case-study link

---

## 2. CONTENT QUALITY GATE

Before publishing:

### FACT
Is the claim directly supported?

### SOURCE
Can the evidence be traced?

### CURRENTNESS
Could the information have changed?

### BUSINESS RELEVANCE
Does it solve or clarify a real business problem?

### ACTION
Can the viewer do something with it?

### CTA
Is there a logical next step?

If a critical answer is no, revise or do not publish.

---

## 3. CONTENT TYPES

### TYPE 01 — PROBLEM
Expose an expensive or frustrating business process.

### TYPE 02 — INTELLIGENCE
Explain what AI can actually do and under which constraints.

### TYPE 03 — DECISION
Explain the trade-offs without fake rankings.

### TYPE 04 — WORKFLOW
Show the actual operating sequence.

### TYPE 05 — BUILD
Show implementation.

### TYPE 06 — MEASUREMENT
Show how success is measured.

### TYPE 07 — OUTCOME
Show documented business change.

### TYPE 08 — LESSON
Show what failed, why, and what changed.

The last type is important: AIMPACT should be credible enough to show failures rather than hide them.

## 4. CONTENT TO SALES ATTRIBUTION

Every channel link should use a controlled campaign/source structure.

Minimum:
- source
- medium
- campaign
- content ID

Website events should capture:
- page_view
- content_click
- workflow_start
- audit_start
- lead_submit
- external_click

Future events:
- discovery_booked
- proposal_sent
- pilot_started
- implementation_started
- outcome_recorded
- case_study_published

---

## 5. CONTENT INTELLIGENCE

After sufficient data exists, classify content by:

- attention
- qualified traffic
- business-problem submissions
- leads
- paid pilots
- revenue
- outcomes

Do not optimize solely for views.

A post with fewer views but qualified pipeline may be more commercially useful than a high-view entertainment post.

---

## 6. FIRST 10 CORE CONTENT INVESTIGATIONS

These are starting themes, not claims that results already exist:

1. Where e-commerce teams lose time between product arrival and content publication.
2. What parts of fashion product-content production can realistically be automated.
3. AI customer-service workflow: where automation should stop and humans should take over.
4. AI-assisted competitor/research workflow with evidence controls.
5. Product image workflow: generation, retouching, QA, publishing.
6. Social-content production workflow from one source asset.
7. Reporting workflow: manual collection → structured intelligence.
8. AI tool pricing/limit verification: why “free” often needs qualification.
9. Workflow automation failure modes and recovery design.
10. A real AIMPACT customer workflow once the first paid pilot exists.

The tenth item must remain empty until a real customer exists.

---

## 7. CONTENT ENGINE RULE

**Do not manufacture authority. Build authority from evidence.**

Every successful implementation should generate:
- one internal workflow
- one customer-facing result
- one case-study asset
- multiple social assets
- one reusable product component

That is how content compounds into company value.
