# AIMPACT Editorial Market Signals — 2026-10-23

## Decision
Lead with specific curiosity, brand/history, comparison, and evidence—not generic AI explainers. Use six editorial pillars: healthspan/longevity; parenting; stocks/funds; premium beauty/aesthetics/fitness; fine wine and spirits; haute horlogerie. This is an editorial portfolio hypothesis, not a claim that analytics have already proven these categories win on the user's Naver blog.

## Evidence observed
1. Chrono24's 2026 report, based on its own completed transactions from 2018 through Q2 2026, says Rolex remains the platform's leading brand but market share has narrowed since 2023 as Cartier, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet and Vacheron Constantin gained ground. It also reports movement among younger buyers toward classic dress watches. Source: https://about.chrono24.com/en/press/The-Rolex-Report-2026-the-same-leader-a-different-market
2. Chrono24's February 2026 review reports 2025 transaction trends including rising demand for rectangular cases (+9.3%), moon-phase complications (+15.3%), and strong growth for Vacheron Constantin (+13.4%) and IWC (+14.4%) on its marketplace. These figures describe that platform's transactions and are not a global census. Source: https://about.chrono24.com/en/press/2025-Watch-Market-Review-Stability-Returns-Speculation-Fades-and-%2522Elegance%2522-Becomes-the-New-Driver-of-Growth
3. BCG's 2025 luxury analysis says the top-tier client segment represents a small share of consumers but a disproportionate share of value, and points to wellness/longevity and beauty as areas of growing interest among wealthy consumers. Treat as research from its defined luxury-consumer sample, not universal demand. Source: https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/the-way-forward-for-luxury-starts-at-the-core
4. Google Think's February 2025 consumer journey analysis describes discovery through streaming, scrolling, searching and shopping, reinforcing that content must win attention and answer active intent across multiple touchpoints. Source: https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/next/article/consumer-insights/new-consumer-decision-making-process/
5. Chubb's July 2026 survey of affluent younger collectors says motivations include enjoyment, status/prestige, expertise and emotional connection, and reports strong digital research alongside preference for in-person sourcing. This supports explaining the story and provenance behind luxury goods rather than only presenting price lists. Source: https://chubb.mediaroom.com/2026-07-16-Chubb-Survey-Finds-78-of-Young-Luxury-Collectors-Buy-for-Investment-But-More-Than-Half-Remain-Uninsured

## What this does and does not prove
- Supported: watch market interest is not only about a single logo; design, heritage, model identity, collector preference, and shifting demand are useful article angles.
- Supported: affluent luxury consumers can care about craft, heritage, wellness, experience, and emotional meaning in addition to price.
- Not yet proven for AIMPACT: which of the six pillars earns the most Korean Naver impressions, click-through, read time, subscribers, or revenue. No connected Naver analytics were accessed in this run.
- Do not invent Google Trends values or call editorial intuition a measured ranking.

## Editorial priority for first test
A. Haute horlogerie: “롤렉스만 찾던 시계 시장, 왜 까르띠에와 바쉐론 콘스탄틴이 다시 주목받나?” Use Chrono24 marketplace data with its limitations and explain history, design, movement, and collector taste.
B. Fine wine/spirits: “오래 숙성한 위스키는 무조건 더 비쌀까? 숙성 연수와 희소성의 함정.” Use producer/region sources, production details, provenance and lawful responsible-alcohol framing.
C. Healthspan: “장수에 좋다는 방법, 실제 사람 연구에서는 어디까지 확인됐나?” Use peer-reviewed human studies and public health authorities; separate evidence from claims.
D. Stocks/funds: “수익률 1위 펀드가 내게도 좋은 펀드일까?” Compare fees, benchmark, risk, drawdown, period and survivorship bias; not personalized investment advice.
E. Beauty/aesthetics: “비싼 시술일수록 효과가 좋을까? 효과·위험·회복기간을 따로 봐야 하는 이유.” Use clinical evidence and official safety guidance.
F. Parenting: “아이 발달에 좋다는 조언, 연구 결과와 SNS 유행은 어떻게 다를까?” Use developmental research and age context; avoid parent shaming.

## Testing plan
For each pillar, publish 3 articles with distinct title angles (curiosity/history, comparison/decision, myth-vs-evidence). Record per-post URL, date, impressions, clicks/CTR where available, average reading time, saves/comments, outbound clicks, qualified leads, and conversions at 7/30 days. Compare medians per pillar; avoid declaring a winner from one viral post or small samples. Use Naver analytics and platform-native data only where actually accessible.

## Standard for every article
- Reader-facing title with a concrete question/tension.
- Opening that creates a genuine information gap without clickbait deception.
- Detailed brand/product/science/market history and why people prefer it.
- Primary sources and publication dates; counterargument and limitations.
- Original visual with a clear purpose; text labels should be added in layout, not baked into AI-generated images.
- One clean paste-ready body; image placement marked explicitly; hashtags listed separately.
- No claim of publication without a public URL; no claim of performance without analytics.
