# AIMPACT NAVER Blog Content Standard

Status: Policy specification only. This document does not itself publish to Naver Blog or prove that an automation pipeline is deployed.

## 1. Editorial scope
Support useful, evidence-led explainers across health, skin care and beauty, fitness, aging, childcare and education, stocks and global capital markets, and other practical life topics. Select topics based on a clearly stated reader question and a credible opportunity to help.

## 2. Evidence and factual integrity
- Prefer primary sources: official agencies, regulators, original research, official datasets, company filings, and original announcements.
- Record source title, publisher, publication/update date, URL, and the exact claim supported.
- Verify time-sensitive prices, schedules, statistics, market data, and policy details as close to publication as practical.
- Clearly label estimates, assumptions, conflicting evidence, and facts that could not be independently verified. Never invent citations, numbers, credentials, or firsthand experience.
- Health, cosmetic procedures, childcare, and investment content must use extra caution, avoid personalized diagnosis or financial advice, and include relevant risk/context notes.
- Do not present this standard as a guarantee of Naver ranking. C-Rank/D.I.A.+ are optimization considerations, not controllable ranking switches.

## 3. Search intent and original value
- Identify the primary search question and answer it early.
- Use a specific, accurate title; clear headings; short paragraphs; lists or comparison tables where useful.
- Add original synthesis, decision criteria, worked examples, or a practical checklist. Do not copy competitors or paraphrase sources without attribution.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, misleading headlines, fabricated reviews, and repetitive low-value posts.

## 4. Visual assets
For each post, provide a visual plan: asset type, placement, purpose, and source/license. Prefer original diagrams or tables where they add clarity. Do not use an image unless its provenance and permitted use are established. If no suitable asset is verified, omit it rather than imply it is licensed.

## 5. Conversion and CTA
Close with a practical next step. Mention AIMPACT Quick Audit only when relevant to the article and describe its scope accurately. Do not imply guaranteed outcomes or invent customer results. Clearly disclose any commercial relationship where applicable.

## 6. Required content package
Before publication, prepare:
1. Search-intent summary and target reader.
2. Proposed title and concise opening answer.
3. Article body with source-linked claims.
4. Source register with dates and URLs.
5. Visual placement and licensing plan.
6. Risk/sensitivity review.
7. Relevant CTA.
8. Publication status and verification evidence.

## 7. Publication gate
Use explicit statuses only: PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT VERIFIED.
- Draft ready is not the same as published.
- A scheduled post is not the same as published.
- Publication is PASS only after the live Naver URL is opened and the title/body are verified.
- Do not claim automatic publication unless the integration, authorization, execution log, and live post have been verified.
- Never store passwords, API secrets, or personal information in source files.
- Any paid API, subscription, or personal-data step requires user approval.

## 8. Automation implementation requirements
This document is an editorial specification, not executable automation. A pipeline must separately implement topic discovery, source retrieval, claim/source mapping, draft generation, validation, visual asset checks, approval gating, Naver-compatible publishing, and post-publication verification. If direct Naver publishing is unsupported or unauthorized, stop at a draft/review queue and state the blocker.
