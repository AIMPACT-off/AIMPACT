# AIMPACT SOCIAL MEDIA OPERATING SYSTEM

## PURPOSE

Instagram and YouTube are not separate marketing projects.

They are acquisition layers inside the AIMPACT business system.

**INSTAGRAM → DISCOVERY**
**YOUTUBE → PROOF**
**WEBSITE → CONVERSION**
**AI AUDIT → QUALIFICATION**
**PAID PILOT → REVENUE**
**OUTCOME → CASE STUDY**
**CASE STUDY → STRONGER ACQUISITION**

---

## 1. INSTAGRAM

### Role
Make a business owner recognize a problem they already have.

### Content mix

**40% Business Problem**
- “이 업무에 사람이 왜 이렇게 많이 붙어 있을까?”
- repetitive work
- slow content production
- customer-response bottlenecks
- merchandising/research friction
- reporting/manual data work

**25% Workflow**
- before → after
- process maps
- automation sequences
- human vs AI responsibility

**20% AI Intelligence**
- verified tool facts
- pricing/limits
- API/integration reality
- commercial-use considerations

**10% Experiments / Proof**
- AIMPACT tests
- measured comparisons
- implementation findings

**5% Company**
- AIMPACT direction
- product building
- selected operating lessons

### Format hierarchy
1. Reels: attention and problem recognition
2. Carousels: saveable intelligence
3. Stories: operating context / questions / responses
4. Static posts: brand-level statements only when they carry real meaning

### Instagram rule
Every post must answer at least one:
- What problem?
- What evidence?
- What decision?
- What workflow?
- What result?

---

## 2. YOUTUBE

### Role
Turn curiosity into trust.

### Series A — AI BUSINESS BREAKDOWN
One real business process, analyzed from problem to workflow.

### Series B — BUILD THE WORKFLOW
Show the actual construction and implementation logic.

### Series C — AI TOOL VERIFICATION
Investigate pricing, limits, API, integrations, commercial use and practical fit.

### Series D — BUSINESS AI AUDIT
Show how AIMPACT analyzes a company/process. Real customer cases only when permission and evidence exist.

### Series E — OUTCOME
Before/after measurement and lessons.

### Series F — AIMPACT SYSTEM
Explain major product/system decisions when useful to customers.

### Long-form standard
Do not make “AI news” the core format.

A YouTube episode should ideally contain:
**Problem → Evidence → Decision → Build → Test → Result → Lesson.**

---

## 3. WEEKLY OPERATING CADENCE

The exact volume should be adjusted by measured capacity and results.

Baseline production system:
- Instagram: 3–5 high-quality pieces/week
- YouTube: 1 substantive video/week
- Stories: operating/customer/problem signals as available

Quality gate:
No forced post.

If there is no evidence or useful insight, do not manufacture one.

---

## 4. ONE IDEA → MULTI-CHANNEL

One substantive AIMPACT investigation becomes:

1. YouTube long-form
2. Instagram Reel
3. Instagram carousel
4. Story sequence
5. Website intelligence/workflow page
6. AI Audit CTA
7. Internal sales asset
8. Future case-study material

The source work should be created once and distributed many times.

---

## 5. VISUAL SYSTEM

AIMPACT visual direction:
- premium technology
- editorial
- high contrast
- restrained typography
- system diagrams
- clean data presentation
- real interface/process evidence

Avoid:
- generic AI humanoids
- neon “future AI” clichés
- stock corporate handshakes
- excessive gradients
- fake dashboards
- fabricated screenshots
- exaggerated performance claims

Instagram thumbnails and YouTube thumbnails should feel like one brand.

---

## 6. VOICE

AIMPACT speaks:
- clearly
- precisely
- calmly
- commercially
- confidently when evidence exists
- cautiously when evidence is incomplete

Preferred:
“확인된 조건에서는 이 방식이 가능합니다.”

Avoid:
“무조건 최고입니다.”
“이 AI 하나면 모든 것이 해결됩니다.”
“100배 생산성.”
“업계 1위” unless independently substantiated.

---

## 9. GOVERNANCE

No social post may:
- invent a customer
- invent a result
- imply a tool is verified when it is not
- imply revenue that has not occurred
- present a hypothesis as fact
- disclose customer information without authorization

Real customer proof requires:
- permission
- documented evidence
- defined before/after measurement
- reproducible source records
