# AIMPACT COMPANY DIRECTION 2030

## 1. NORTH STAR

AIMPACT is not an AI news account, AI tool directory, chatbot wrapper, or consulting brochure.

AIMPACT is building an **AI Decision & Execution Layer for Business**.

Positioning:
> **AI CURATES THE FUTURE.**

Operating promise:
> **Find the business problem. Turn intelligence into a decision. Turn the decision into a workflow. Execute it. Measure the result. Learn from the outcome.**

Core loop:
**PROBLEM → INTELLIGENCE → DECISION → WORKFLOW → EXECUTION → RESULT → OUTCOME DATA → PRODUCT**

The long-term moat is not a list of AI tools. It is accumulated evidence about which AI decisions and workflows produce measurable business outcomes.

---

## 2. WHAT AIMPACT MUST BECOME

### Stage A — Intelligence
Build trusted, evidence-backed intelligence about AI capabilities, pricing, limits, integrations, commercial use, and practical business fit.

### Stage B — Decision
Understand a company's problem and recommend an evidence-backed decision path without pretending that a generic “best tool” exists.

### Stage C — Execution
Turn the decision into a concrete workflow, implementation, automation, operating procedure, and measurement plan.

### Stage D — Business System
Monitor execution, detect friction, handle incidents, improve workflows, and connect operational results to business KPIs.

### Stage E — AI Operating Layer
AIMPACT becomes infrastructure through which businesses continuously discover, decide, execute, measure, and improve AI-enabled work.

## 8. CHANNEL SYSTEM

### Instagram
Role: **DISCOVERY**

Instagram earns the first 3–10 seconds of attention.

Primary formats:
- business problem breakdowns
- AI workflow before/after
- short tool intelligence
- operational mistakes
- visual process maps
- AI myths vs evidence
- short case evidence once real cases exist

CTA:
**“문제를 보내주세요.”**
→ AIMPACT website → AI Audit / Workflow.

### YouTube
Role: **PROOF**

YouTube earns trust through depth.

Primary formats:
- real workflow builds
- AI Business Audit breakdowns
- tool verification investigations
- business process transformations
- implementation experiments
- customer case studies
- long-form operating-system explanations

CTA:
**“AIMPACT에서 귀사의 업무를 진단하세요.”**
→ Website → Audit → Lead → Paid Pilot.

### Website
Role: **CONVERSION**

The website is not a brochure.

Primary jobs:
1. Explain the category.
2. Demonstrate intelligence.
3. Show workflows.
4. Capture a real business problem.
5. Convert the problem into a lead.
6. Support delivery and measurement.

### Email / CRM
Role: **RELATIONSHIP**

Every real lead should enter a controlled lifecycle:
Prospect → Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Paid Pilot → Customer → Outcome → Case Study → Expansion.

## 10. CONTENT RULE

Do not publish because a calendar says “post today.”

Publish when one of these exists:
- a useful business insight
- verified intelligence
- a workflow
- a measured experiment
- a real customer problem
- a real result
- a meaningful system lesson

Content is an acquisition mechanism, not the product.

## 14. 2030 NORTH STAR

AIMPACT should eventually answer:

> “What business problem are you trying to solve?”

Then:
1. Understand the process.
2. Retrieve relevant evidence.
3. Evaluate feasible AI approaches.
4. Recommend a decision path.
5. Generate a workflow.
6. Execute or coordinate implementation.
7. Monitor the result.
8. Measure the business outcome.
9. Learn from the outcome.
10. Improve the next decision.

That is the company.

**AIMPACT is not building a better list of AI tools. AIMPACT is building the operating layer between business problems and AI-powered outcomes.**
