## CONTENT ARCHITECTURE

### Instagram

Role: **DISCOVERY + PROBLEM RECOGNITION**

Content mix:
- 35% business problems
- 25% workflows
- 15% verified AI intelligence
- 10% experiments/proof
- 10% company/product building
- 5% documented lessons/failures

Formats:
- Reels: attention and demonstration
- Carousels: saveable intelligence
- Stories: operating signals, questions, proof and distribution
- Static: high-value brand statements only

Baseline:
- 3–5 quality posts/week
- Stories on operating days
- no forced posting

### YouTube

Role: **PROOF + TRUST + DEMONSTRATION**

Series:
1. AI BUSINESS BREAKDOWN
2. BUILD THE WORKFLOW
3. AI TOOL VERIFICATION
4. BUSINESS AI AUDIT
5. OUTCOME
6. AIMPACT SYSTEM

Baseline:
- 1 substantive long-form/week
- Shorts derived from real source work
- no AI-news treadmill

### ONE INVESTIGATION → MANY ASSETS

One verified investigation should produce:
- 1 YouTube episode
- 1–2 Reels
- 1 carousel
- 3–7 Story frames
- 1 website intelligence/workflow asset
- 1 sales/audit asset
- 1 internal learning record

The source investigation is the canonical content record. Derivatives never become separate facts.

## CONTENT QUALITY GATE

Before publication:

FACT → SOURCE → CURRENTNESS → BUSINESS RELEVANCE → ACTION → CTA → BRAND CHECK

Required status:
- VERIFIED: publish as fact
- HYPOTHESIS: label as hypothesis
- BLOCKED: do not publish
- APPROVAL_REQUIRED: stop
- LIVE: only after publication evidence

No content claim may imply:
- customer success without a customer record
- revenue without a transaction
- verified AI capability without evidence
- performance without measurement

**TRUST → DEMAND → CUSTOMER → OUTCOME → REVENUE → PRODUCT VALUE**
