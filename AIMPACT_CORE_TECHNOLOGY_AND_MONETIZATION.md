# AIMPACT — CORE TECHNOLOGY & MONETIZATION SYSTEM

## 1. The Company in One Sentence

AIMPACT converts real business problems into verified AI decisions, executable workflows, measurable outcomes, and recurring operating systems.

AIMPACT is not primarily selling AI tools.
AIMPACT is selling **business outcomes produced through a controlled AI decision-and-execution layer**.

---

## 2. The Core Problem AIMPACT Owns

Businesses do not fundamentally need more AI tools.

They need to answer:

1. What problem is costing us money or time?
2. Where exactly is the cause?
3. Which AI capability can solve it?
4. Which tool/model/workflow is actually suitable?
5. What will it cost?
6. Can it integrate with the existing business?
7. How should it be implemented?
8. Did it actually improve the business?
9. Can the improvement be repeated automatically?

AIMPACT owns this chain:

**BUSINESS PROBLEM → EVIDENCE → AI INTELLIGENCE → DECISION → WORKFLOW → EXECUTION → MEASUREMENT → OUTCOME → LEARNING**

---

## 3. AIMPACT's Most Important Proprietary Capability

The strategic moat is not a list of AI tools.

It is the **AIMPACT Business Decision & Execution Engine**.

The engine should continuously connect five layers:

### Layer A — Business Problem Graph
Maps:
- industry
- company
- process
- bottleneck
- cost
- revenue opportunity
- customer problem
- operational failure
- measurable KPI

### Layer B — AI Capability & Verification Graph
Maps:
- AI tools
- models
- APIs
- integrations
- pricing
- free/paid limits
- commercial-use conditions
- reliability
- current evidence
- compatibility
- limitations

### Layer C — Decision Engine
Determines, from evidence:
- what should be automated
- what should remain human-controlled
- which AI capability fits
- which workflow is appropriate
- expected effort
- measurable success condition
- risk and approval requirements

The engine must explain the evidence behind a recommendation.

### Layer D — Workflow / Execution Engine
Converts the decision into:
- workflow steps
- integrations
- automation
- human approval gates
- monitoring
- error handling
- rollback
- result verification

### Layer E — Outcome Learning Layer
Records:
- before/after KPI
- time saved
- cost saved
- revenue impact
- conversion impact
- error reduction
- customer response
- workflow success/failure
- implementation cost

The outcome feeds back into the decision system.

This creates a compounding loop:

**PROBLEM → DECISION → EXECUTION → OUTCOME → DATA → BETTER DECISION**

---

## 4. What AIMPACT Actually Owns

AIMPACT should progressively own the decision layer between a business and the rapidly changing AI market.

The customer should not need to understand hundreds or thousands of AI products.

The customer states a business problem.

AIMPACT determines:

**PROBLEM → WHAT AI CAN DO → WHICH OPTION → WHY → HOW → EXECUTE → MEASURE**

This is the central product.

## 5. Monetization Objective

The first objective is NOT advertising revenue.

The first objective is to convert verified intelligence into paid business execution.

### Revenue Layer 1 — AI Business Audit

Paid diagnosis of a company's:
- operations
- marketing
- content
- sales
- customer service
- repetitive work
- AI readiness
- automation opportunities

Output:
- problem map
- opportunity map
- AI recommendations
- workflow designs
- priority actions
- expected measurement framework

### Revenue Layer 2 — Paid AI Pilot

Take one high-value problem and implement it.

Examples:
- content production workflow
- product description workflow
- customer-service automation
- lead qualification
- sales follow-up
- reporting automation
- internal knowledge workflow

The pilot must have:
- baseline
- implementation
- KPI
- result
- evidence

### Revenue Layer 3 — Implementation

Charge for building the approved system:
- workflow
- integrations
- automations
- dashboards
- data pipelines
- approval gates
- monitoring
- QA

### Revenue Layer 4 — Recurring AI Operations

Monthly recurring revenue for:
- monitoring
- optimization
- AI/tool verification
- workflow maintenance
- performance analysis
- new automation opportunities
- incident response
- continuous improvement

This turns one project into recurring revenue.

### Revenue Layer 5 — AIMPACT Platform

After sufficient real-world data exists, productize repeated work into software.

Potential pricing:
- subscription
- usage-based
- workflow-based
- seat/team-based
- API
- enterprise license

The platform can provide:
- business problem diagnosis
- verified AI matching
- decision recommendations
- workflow generation
- execution monitoring
- outcome dashboards

### Revenue Layer 6 — Data / Intelligence Products

Only after sufficient verified proprietary data exists:

- AI capability intelligence
- business automation benchmarks
- industry workflow intelligence
- AI implementation benchmarks
- verified AI/tool datasets
- enterprise intelligence/API access

No data product should be sold merely because a database exists. It requires sufficient freshness, provenance, accuracy, and commercial value.

### Revenue Layer 7 — Transaction / Partner Revenue

Where commercially and legally appropriate:
- qualified leads
- implementation partners
- software referrals
- integration partners
- enterprise procurement
- transaction fees

This is supplementary, not the strategic core.
