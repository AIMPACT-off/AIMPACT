# AIMPACT Brand Film — 60-second storyboard
Status: PRE-PRODUCTION — actual product footage required
Master line: WE DON'T SELL AI. WE TURN BUSINESS PROBLEMS INTO MEASURABLE OUTCOMES.

| Time | Narrative | Visual / evidence requirement | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Businesses do not need more AI noise. They need to find the work that is breaking. | Real workspace footage or licensed, non-identifying material. | BUSINESS PROBLEMS ARE THE STARTING POINT. |
| 5–13s | Find a repeatable process and establish a baseline. | Actual workflow mapping; sample data must be labeled SAMPLE. | DISCOVER THE BOTTLENECK. |
| 13–25s | AIMPACT structures the problem, checks evidence and compares possible approaches. | Capture actual running app/workflow. Record commit/build and capture date. | DISCOVER · VERIFY · COMPARE |
| 25–37s | Turn the selected approach into an approved workflow with human checkpoints. | Show real controls and approval step; do not invent UI. | APPLY WITH CONTROL. |
| 37–47s | Measure the result against the baseline. | Show verified metrics only. If no customer result exists, show the measurement method instead. | MEASURE WHAT CHANGED. |
| 47–54s | Keep exceptions visible and outcomes traceable. | Real log/exception handling screen with secrets and personal data hidden. | OBSERVE · VERIFY · IMPROVE |
| 54–60s | Invite the buyer to start with a focused audit. | Final title card and approved CTA URL. | WE DON'T SELL AI. WE TURN BUSINESS PROBLEMS INTO MEASURABLE OUTCOMES. / QUICK AUDIT ₩200,000 |

## Capture checklist
- [ ] Confirm product build SHA, app version and environment.
- [ ] Record actual screen capture; no mock UI presented as live.
- [ ] Use synthetic/demo data only when clearly labeled.
- [ ] Remove credentials, customer PII and private operational identifiers.
- [ ] Verify every displayed metric against a source and time window.
- [ ] Confirm landing page and payment destination work before CTA is rendered.
- [ ] Save source footage, final export, caption/subtitles and approval record.
- [ ] Do not claim publishing or performance until platform logs confirm it.
