# AIMPACT Instagram Conversion Playbook — 2026-10-12

## Purpose
Turn Instagram discovery into qualified business enquiries. AIMPACT is an AI Business Operations company that diagnoses real workflows, chooses evidence-backed AI options, helps implement the workflow, and measures results. It is not positioned as a generic AI tool directory.

## Public positioning
**AIMPACT — AI BUSINESS OPERATIONS**
We find operational leaks, design practical AI workflows, and measure what changes.
**Core line:** FIND THE LEAK. FIX WHAT PAYS.
**Truth rule:** No evidence, no claim. No measured baseline, no ROI promise.

## Who should care
- E-commerce and retail operators: product data, catalog work, customer questions, campaign reporting.
- Fashion and consumer brands: product imagery, launch calendars, content production and inventory/reporting workflows.
- Marketing/content teams: research, repurposing, approval workflows and performance reporting.
- Operations leaders and founders: repetitive handoffs, spreadsheet work, fragmented tools and slow decisions.

## What a customer actually gets
1. **Discover:** describe one recurring, costly or slow process.
2. **Diagnose:** map steps, people, systems, bottlenecks and risks.
3. **Verify & compare:** shortlist suitable AI options using evidence and constraints.
4. **Design:** define the workflow, human approval points, integrations and failure handling.
5. **Implement:** scope a pilot before claiming a capability is live.
6. **Measure:** compare actual baseline and after-state for time, cost, quality or throughput.
7. **Repeat:** maintain and improve workflows when the evidence supports it.

## Reel structure (20–25 seconds)
- 0–3s: “Your team doesn’t need more AI tools. It needs fewer workflow leaks.”
- 3–7s: Show one concrete process: manual product information → image/content approval → publish.
- 7–12s: On-screen sequence: DISCOVER → DIAGNOSE → VERIFY.
- 12–17s: Show workflow map / human review gate / measurement fields. Use a real interface only if verified; otherwise use a clearly illustrative diagram.
- 17–21s: “Design the workflow. Measure the change.”
- 21–25s: AIMPACT brand end card + “Describe one process via the profile link.”
- Captions must be burned in, high contrast, concise, and inside vertical-video safe margins.

## First 6 specific content pieces
1. **Workflow leak:** “Why product content gets stuck between arrival, information, approval and publishing.” Show the handoffs; do not claim quantified savings.
2. **Before you automate:** “Map the 5 steps before choosing an AI tool.” Include input, output, owner, exception, and quality check.
3. **Human-in-the-loop:** “What AI should draft—and what a person must approve.” Use product claims, customer replies and brand-sensitive assets as examples.
4. **Evidence over hype:** “How AIMPACT decides whether an AI tool is fit for a workflow.” Show capability, limits, price, integration, privacy and verification status.
5. **Measurement:** “What to record before an automation pilot.” Capture cycle time, manual touches, error/rework rate and output volume before and after.
6. **Company walkthrough:** “From one business problem to an implementation plan.” Demonstrate intake → diagnosis → prioritized action plan → measurement plan. Clearly distinguish prototype/demo from production.

## Photo/video production quality gate
- Reels: 1080×1920, 9:16; key text inside safe margins; subtitles reviewed for accuracy.
- Feed/carousel: 1080×1350, 4:5; one claim per frame, strong hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Stories: 1080×1920; one action per frame and a clear link/CTA.
- Use documentary-quality real work environments, close-ups of hands/screens/materials, restrained typography and controlled lighting. Avoid generic AI robots, fake customer dashboards, fake testimonials and unverified metrics.
- Keep a source asset ID, rights/consent note, claim/evidence status, edit version, export settings, caption, thumbnail and destination URL for every asset.
- Reuse one verified investigation across a Reel, carousel, Stories and website explainer; never invent new facts in derivatives.

## Lead form requirements before launch
